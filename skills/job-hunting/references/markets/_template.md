# Market file template — the headings every market fills

Every `markets/<cc>.md` uses the headings below, numbered and in this
order, so `<cc>.md` §N means the same section in every market. Filled
files: `my.md` (Malaysia) and `sg.md` (Singapore). Load the user's market
file before any market-sensitive advice; never assume US conventions.

## Opening line

Each market file opens with its compiled date and the re-verify note:

`Compiled YYYY-MM; statutory figures drift — re-verify any number before quoting it precisely.`

## Headings, in order

| § | Heading | What goes here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Boards & channels | Volume board, mid-senior channel, niche/tech boards, government portal; which ones `search_jobs.py` covers; board ranking mechanics; market pulse |
| 2 | Agencies | The agencies that matter, as a list; contract and temp roles; etiquette stays in `networking.md` §6 |
| 3 | Salary sources | Salary guides, salary sites, posted-band conventions and benchmark medians, each with source and date |
| 4 | Resume conventions | Length, photo (with the photo decision matrix in `resume-revamp.md`), national ID numbers, expected salary on the CV |
| 5 | Application form norms | Board form mechanics (the expected-salary field), common screening questions, what never goes in a screening field |
| 6 | Statutory floors | Minimum wage, hours and overtime, leave, statutory contributions as a table, then contract red flags |
| 7 | Probation & notice | Typical probation; notice in and after probation vs the statutory default; how confirmation is documented |
| 8 | 13th month / bonus wording | Mandatory or not; the wording that makes it contractual vs discretionary; contribution caps on bonuses |
| 9 | Work authorization signals | Work-status wording for CV and forms; pass rules and per-role viability checks for non-citizens |
| 10 | Interview process, curveballs & rights | Process: typical rounds and order, timeline, assessments, salary-expectation handling, notice talk, language, AI-tool policy, source; then personal questions that still get asked, what the law says about them, the right to decline |
| 11 | Recourse | Where wage, contract, dismissal and discrimination complaints go |

## Any other country — fill it from a live search

1. Search each heading live; prefer government, statute and board sources
   over listicles. Cite every fact with its source and date in
   parentheses.
2. Label anything no dated source confirms "(unverified)". Never fill a
   statutory number from memory; leave the cell as "look up" instead.
3. Confirm each board is still live — round-ups go stale: StackOverflow
   Jobs closed in 2022 and Tech in Asia shut its Indonesia site in July
   2025, yet older lists still name them.
4. Start from the brief below when the country has one, then extend it.
5. Give the user the filled headings inline, opened with the compiled
   line; never write into the skill's `references/`.
6. Contract, visa or legal questions: general information, then suggest a
   qualified professional (SKILL.md).

## Brief examples — PH, ID, VN

**Philippines (PH)** — Boards: JobStreet, Kalibrr (tech and startup,
skills-verified), LinkedIn. Resume: a 2x2 photo is still conventional at
local firms, declining at MNCs. 13th month: **mandatory** for
rank-and-file employees, paid by 24 December. (v5 notes, July 2026 —
re-verify)

**Indonesia (ID)** — Boards: JobStreet, Glints (tech and junior),
Kalibrr. Probation: at most 3 months on permanent contracts. Bonus: THR,
the religious holiday allowance of about one month, is the 13th-month
equivalent — verify the specifics before quoting them. (v5 notes, July
2026 — re-verify)

**Vietnam (VN)** — Boards: ITviec (curated IT), TopDev (large developer
community), VietnamWorks (general); referrals often beat boards.
Probation: up to 60 days for degree-required roles, 180 for managerial
roles. Notice after probation: 30–45 days. (v5 notes, July 2026 —
re-verify)
