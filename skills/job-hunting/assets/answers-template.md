## Tell me about yourself
> 60-90 seconds, spoken: present (one line) -> proof (strongest relevant
> evidence from profile section 5) -> why here. Tailor the last line per
> company. Every claim comes from the profile (facts-policy section 1).
-

## Strength and weakness
> Strength with evidence from profile section 5, not adjectives. Weakness: a
> real one plus the fix in progress - never a humblebrag. Every claim comes
> from the profile (facts-policy section 1).
-

## Pressure and stress
> A system, not a claim: trigger -> mechanism -> one real example -> result.
> The example and result come from the profile (facts-policy section 1).
-
