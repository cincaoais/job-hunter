# SEA Market Notes — Malaysia & Singapore focus

Practical norms for the user's market, extending the Application Guide's
"Market norms vary" section. Compiled July 2026 from web research; norms and
platform mechanics drift — re-verify anything time-sensitive (UI mechanics,
fees, statutory ceilings) with a quick search before giving precise advice.

## JobStreet expected-salary field

- Platform context: SEEK unified the JobStreet/JobsDB platforms in Feb 2024;
  one matching engine now sits behind the MY/SG/PH/ID brands.
- The structured profile field takes a **single number**, which the platform
  matches against the employer's posted band — and employers filter on it.
  The common "give a range" advice applies to interviews and free-text
  fields, not this field.
- Practical play: set the single number near the bottom of the user's true
  range so band-matching doesn't exclude them, and keep the full range for
  the interview. Append "(negotiable)" where free text allows. Leaving it
  blank (when optional) is legitimate if the user prefers not to disclose.
- Exact current UI labels are unverified — don't describe precise screens.

## Probation, notice, 13th month — Malaysia

- **Probation:** no statutory length; 3 months typical, 6 for senior or
  specialized roles. Must be stated in the contract; confirmation requires a
  letter — it is not automatic.
- **Notice:** during probation often 1–2 weeks per contract. Confirmed
  roles: ~1 month junior, 2–3 months senior/lead. Employment Act defaults
  (4/6/8 weeks by tenure) apply only when the contract is silent.
- **13th month:** not mandated; binding only if contractual. Job-ad cue:
  "guaranteed/contractual" = real 13th month, "discretionary / subject to
  performance" = bonus. Tech MNCs and startups often pay performance bonus
  instead — worth asking at offer stage.

## Probation, notice, AWS — Singapore

- **Probation:** not regulated by the Employment Act; 3–6 months typical.
  Terms must appear in the Key Employment Terms (KETs). CPF applies during
  probation like after it.
- **Notice:** statutory defaults scale with tenure (1 day → 4 weeks) but
  contracts govern in practice: 1–3 months once confirmed, longer for
  senior. TAFEP flags long notice for junior roles as unfair practice.
- **AWS (13th month):** not mandatory; typically one month's basic salary in
  Nov/Dec; becomes binding once in the contract. CPF Additional Wage
  Ceiling: S$102,000 (2025) rising to S$110,800 (2026).

## Brief: Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam

- **PH:** 13th-month pay is **mandatory** for rank-and-file (by Dec 24).
  Kalibrr matters for tech. A 2x2 photo is still conventional at local
  firms, declining at MNCs.
- **ID:** probation max 3 months on permanent contracts. THR (religious
  holiday allowance, ~1 month) is the 13th-month equivalent — verify
  specifics if relevant.
- **VN:** probation up to 60 days for degree-required roles (180 for
  managerial); post-probation notice 30–45 days. Referrals often beat
  boards.

## Resume conventions

- **MY:** max ~2 pages. Photo optional for tech roles. Exclude the IC
  number and expected salary from the resume itself.
- **SG:** 1–2 pages. **Never** put the NRIC on a CV — state work status
  instead ("Singapore Citizen", "PR", "EP holder valid until [date]").
  Expected salary only if the ad asks; then a range plus "(negotiable)".
  Photo is a genuinely mixed norm: skip for Western MNCs/tech, harmless at
  local/traditional firms.

## Boards that matter for tech (2025–2026)

- **MY:** JobStreet (volume default) · LinkedIn (mid-senior) · Hiredly
  (junior/startup) · Glints (tech/startup).
- **SG:** JobStreet (all-rounder) · MyCareersFuture (government portal,
  real volume) · LinkedIn (mid-senior, networking) · NodeFlair
  (tech-specific, salary data) · Glints · Indeed.
- **PH:** JobStreet · Kalibrr (tech/startup, skills-verified) · LinkedIn.
- **ID:** JobStreet · Glints (tech/junior) · Kalibrr.
- **VN:** ITviec (curated IT) · TopDev (large dev community) · VietnamWorks
  (general).
- Stale-source warning: StackOverflow Jobs died in 2022 and Tech in Asia
  shut its Indonesia site in July 2025 — round-ups still listing them are
  outdated.

## LinkedIn vs JobStreet split (MY/SG)

JobStreet is the default apply channel across levels; LinkedIn skews
mid-to-senior and is used more for networking, vetting, and recruiter
sourcing than for applying — SEA differs from US norms here. For junior or
local IC roles, don't over-index on LinkedIn ads: keep JobStreet primary and
use LinkedIn for the referral paths the Networking section already treats as
a main pillar.
