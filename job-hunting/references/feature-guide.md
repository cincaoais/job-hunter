# Feature Guide — what this skill can do

Show this menu when the user asks what the skill can do, says "help",
"feature guide", "menu", "what features are there", or clearly forgot a
capability exists. Present the table, then ask which one they want — don't
just dump it and stop.

## The menu

| Feature | Say something like | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Profile setup** | "Set up my profile" / share a resume | The skill's memory of you built from your documents + a few grouped questions. Everything else reads from it. |
| **Job search** | "Find me jobs to apply to this week" | Ranked shortlist from your saved criteria — fit scores, red flags (ghost/scam checks), salary intel, plus recruiter & teammate LinkedIn search links per high-fit role for the referral path. |
| **JD match** | "Am I a fit for this posting?" + paste it | Requirement-by-requirement scorecard, missing keywords vs real gaps, honest apply/stretch/skip verdict, truthful resume edits. |
| **Upskill review** | "What should I learn to land these roles?" | Gap heatmap across the postings you're targeting + a prioritized learning plan with resources, time estimates, and a provable artifact per skill. |
| **Resume / cover letter** | "Tailor my resume for this posting" | Inline draft → your reactions → reviewer pass → ATS-safe .docx/PDF, verified by re-extracting the file's text the way an ATS parser reads it. |
| **Resume revamp & score** | "Revamp my resume" / "it looks plain" / "score my resume like HR would" | 0–100 HR-eye score with impact-ranked example fixes, then a ground-up rebuild: ATS master .docx + a presentation PDF styled to your industry (photo and layout norms decided per market). |
| **Soft skills coach** | "Postings keep asking for X and I'm weak at it" | Demand-vs-supply assessment, small practice plan, progress turned into truthful bullets and STAR stories. |
| **Interview coach** | "Mock interview me for the Grab screen" | One question at a time with real-interviewer probing, honest scoring, debrief that files STAR stories into your profile. |
| **Company dossier** | "Brief me before my interview at X" | One-page cited brief: product, health, stack, comp intel, likely interview themes mapped to your evidence, questions to ask. |
| **Profile optimizer** | "Improve my LinkedIn/JobStreet profile" | Keyword-first truthful headline, About hook, skills order, platform settings — before → after edits. |
| **Pipeline & funnel review** | "How is my search going?" | Sent → response → interview → offer funnel by channel, leak diagnosis, one concrete change to make. |
| **Offer coach** | "I got an offer — should I take it?" + paste it | True annual comp (13th month/AWS/EPF mechanics), side-by-side comparison, negotiate/accept/decline verdict, counter scripts, mock negotiation. |

## Two ground rules worth repeating

- **Your profile is the memory.** Every answer you give is written back to
  `candidate-profile.md`; save the regenerated copy at the end of a session
  and you'll never be asked twice.
- **Honesty guardrail.** Strongest *true* version of your experience —
  never invented metrics, titles, or achievements.
